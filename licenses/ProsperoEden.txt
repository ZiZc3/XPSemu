XPSemu adapts from it (our own C code, under the same GPL-3.0-or-later):

- The write helper (ps5/wbwriter/wbwriter.c, ps5/src/wbwriter_client.c,
  ps5/include/wbwriter_proto.h): streaming a helper ELF to the console's
  payload loader and keeping that socket as the channel to it, so file writes
  run in a process the PS5 does not throttle. From ProsperoEden's
  headless/self_update_helper/ (main.cpp, updater.cpp) and
  headless/update_check/ (self_update_protocol.h, self_update_ps5.c).

- DualSense HD haptics (ps5/src/haptics_ps5.c): driving the controller's
  voice-coil actuators through its vibration audio port, and the haptic
  signal design (low and high sine bands with low-passed noise, enveloped).
  From ProsperoEden's headless/pad_haptics.cpp and pad_haptics.h.
