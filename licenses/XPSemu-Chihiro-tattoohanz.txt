XPSemu-Chihiro (tattoohanz) — study / reference credit
https://github.com/tattoohanz/XPSemu-Chihiro

XPSemu's Sega Chihiro support was written by the XPSemu authors. The
XPSemu-Chihiro fork by tattoohanz was studied as a reference for the Chihiro
hardware model, and its approach informed our own implementation of:

  - the Namco V257 drive board,
  - the CRP-1231 card reader,
  - the media-board network firmware answer used for Wangan Midnight
    Maximum Tune (the network board's firmware-version reply), and
  - the base boards (8051 / AN2131, JVS), game controls and steering-wheel
    force feedback.

Our Chihiro code is our own and is licensed under GPL-3.0 with the rest of
XPSemu. Where XPSemu-Chihiro's approach is adapted, the relevant XPSemu-Chihiro
code is distributed by tattoohanz under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or later; the full text is in LGPL-2.1.txt in this folder.

The Chihiro GD-ROM DES decryption is a separate port from MAME (BSD-3-Clause);
see MAME-DES.txt. Starting Chihiro games without a BIOS follows the method used
by Cxbx-Reloaded.
