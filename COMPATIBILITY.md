# XPSemu Compatibility

Games tested by the developer on a PS5. "FPS" is what the game itself draws; many Xbox games are capped at 30 or 60 FPS.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Great | Runs at full speed. |
| ✅ Good | Fully playable; can dip in heavy scenes. |
| ⚠️ In-game | Plays, but below full speed in places. |

## Tested games

| Game | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Crash Bandicoot: The Wrath of Cortex | ✅ Great | Runs smoothly. |
| Crash Nitro Kart | ✅ Good | 30 FPS most of the time. |
| Fable: The Lost Chapters | ⚠️ In-game | 22-30 FPS in many areas (30 is its cap), 10-20 FPS in heavy areas. |
| Forza Motorsport | ⚠️ In-game | 30 FPS (its cap) in menus and intro, 15-21 FPS while racing. |
| Halo: Combat Evolved | ✅ Great | 30 FPS (its cap). 60 FPS with a 60 FPS patch. |
| Halo 2 | ✅ Good | 30 FPS stock. With the Halo 2 60 FPS patch: 51-60 FPS in the opening, 36-50 in gameplay. |
| Jet Set Radio Future | ✅ Great | 60 FPS. |
| Ninja Gaiden Black | ✅ Great | 60 FPS. |

## Notes

- Games must be **XISO** images (`.iso` / `.xiso`); full "redump" disc images must be converted first.
- Anything that runs well in xemu on PC has a good chance of running here, more slowly in CPU-heavy scenes. xemu's own [compatibility list](https://xemu.app/#compatibility) is a good first check. Games that fail on PC xemu fail here too.
- Tested a game that isn't listed? Open an [issue](https://github.com/ZiZc3/XPSemu/issues) with the game's name, how it runs and your `xemu-game.log`.
